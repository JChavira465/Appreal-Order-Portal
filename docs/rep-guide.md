# Apparel Logic — Rep Guide

_Last updated: September 3, 2026_

This is everything you need to know to take an order, track it, and get paid
using the Apparel Logic.

## Signing in

1. Open the app. Pick your name from the dropdown and enter your 4-digit PIN.
2. Your manager sets up your account and starting PIN. Once you're signed
   in, you can change your PIN any time from the home screen ("Set / change
   your PIN").
3. Trouble signing in? Tap "Trouble signing in?" on the login screen.

## The home screen

- **Signed in as** shows your name and role.
- If you have any orders overdue or due within 7 days, a banner tells you
  right away — tap it to jump to your orders.
- **New Order** — start a new order.
- **My Orders** — everything you've submitted (or saved as a draft).
- **Activity** — a running feed of everything happening on your orders
  (mockup sent, payment recorded, status changed, and so on), most recent
  first, in case you want the news without opening each order.
- **Set / change your PIN.**
- **Report Issue** (bottom-right corner, on every screen) — if something in
  the app looks wrong or breaks, tap this, describe what happened, and it
  goes straight to the developer. No need to text or call.

## Starting a new order

Tap **New Order**. Fill in:

1. **Load saved customer** (optional) — if this team has ordered before,
   pick them here and their name/contact/address/sport auto-fill.
2. **Team / Customer name** — required. If this team already has a real
   order in the last 48 hours, a small warning shows up so you can catch
   an accidental double-entry — it's just a heads-up, it won't stop you
   from submitting a genuinely separate order for the same team.
3. **Contact name** and **Contact phone** — usually the coach.
4. **Shipping address.**
5. **Sport.**
6. **Needed by** — the deadline. Required.

### Adding items

For each item on the order:

- Pick the **item** (jersey, hoodie, hat, etc.) and any **add-ons** (mesh
  type, collar style, and similar options — some are optional, some have a
  default already picked for you).
- Choose **Roster** or **Sizes only** for that item:
  - **Roster** — use this when names/numbers need to be printed on the
    back. Enter each person's name, number, and size, or tap
    **"Paste a list instead"** to bulk-paste a whole team roster at once.
    Any of these formats work, one per line:
    ```
    Smith 23
    Smith, 23
    Smith #23
    ```
    You can also add a size at the end of the line if you're pasting sizes
    too.
  - **Sizes only** — use this for items that don't get a name/number
    printed (extra gear, bags, etc.). Just enter sizes and quantities —
    faster when you don't need a full roster.
  - Sizes go up to **8XL**. Pants/bottoms support a custom waist × length
    (e.g. "34x32") if a standard size doesn't fit.
  - **Load saved roster** / **Save this roster** _(not on every plan)_ —
    if a team orders from
    you every season, save their roster once and load it back on a future
    order instead of retyping every name and number. Saved by team name,
    so it works even the very first time you type a brand-new team's name
    (as long as you use the exact same name next time). Saving replaces
    whatever was saved before for that team, so it always reflects the
    latest roster you saved.
- Add more item blocks with **+ Add item** for a mixed order (jerseys +
  hats + pants, etc.).

### The rest of the form

- **Reference photos** — upload whatever the customer gave you (a logo,
  colorway, an old jersey photo, anything design-related).
- **Notes** — colors, logo placement, anything the office should know.
- **Estimated shipping** — your best guess is fine; the office can correct
  it later once they know the real cost.

### Save draft vs. Submit order

- **Save draft** — only the team name is required. Use this if you're
  filling the order out over multiple sittings, or waiting on info from the
  customer. A draft is private — only you (and managers) can see it, and it
  never shows up on the shop's Order Board until you submit it.
- **Submit order** — needs team name, deadline, and at least one item with
  a size and quantity. Once submitted, it's a real order in the shop's
  queue.

## Editing an order

- You can edit your own order while it's still **Submitted** or a
  **Draft**.
- Once the office starts working on the mockup, it locks — from that point
  on, ask the office to make any changes for you.
- You can cancel your own order yourself, but only while it's still
  "Submitted" (before mockup work starts). After that, only the office can
  cancel it.
- A draft you don't want can be **discarded** outright — it was never a
  real order, so there's nothing to "cancel," it just deletes.

## Order status

Every order moves through the same five stages, shown as a progress bar on
the order:

**Submitted → Mockup Sent → Mockup Approved → In Production → Shipped**

Moving an order forward through these stages is the office's job, not
yours — you'll just watch it progress. The one exception is the mockup
step below, which is on you.

## Reviewing a mockup

When the office sends a mockup for one of your orders, there are two ways
it gets approved:

- **You approve it on the customer's behalf** — check the mockup with your
  customer yourself (call, text a screenshot, show them in person), then
  tap **Approve** or **Revise** on the order page.
- **The customer approves it themselves** — send them the **Customer
  tracking link** (see below). Once a mockup's posted, that same link
  shows them the design and gives them their own Approve / Request
  changes buttons — no login needed on their end. Either path does the
  same thing; use whichever's easier for that customer.

**Revise** (or the customer's "Request changes") just flags the order and
lets the office know to make another pass — it doesn't collect a reason,
so if the customer wants something specific changed, pass that along to
the office separately (a note on the order, a text, whatever's fastest).

## AI concept ideas _(not on every plan)_

Before the office's official mockup, you can generate rough AI concept
images on your own orders for early customer feedback — look for
**"AI concept ideas (not official)"** on the order page. These are just
quick, unofficial previews, not the real design.

If you don't see this on your order page, your shop's plan doesn't
include it — ask whoever runs the shop.

## Payments

Only the office records payments — you'll see **Paid** and **Balance due**
on every order, but recording a payment itself is a manager action.

If there's a balance due, you'll see a **Venmo pay link** you can send
straight to the customer. Pick whichever partner/collector the payment
should go to before sending the link.

## Customer order link _(not on every plan)_

Different from the tracking link below. **The tracking link is for an
order that already exists. This one is for an order that doesn't.**

On your home screen there's a **Customer order link** card. Tap
**Create my link** once and you get a permanent link that's yours — you
only ever need to make it once.

Send that link to a customer and they fill the order out themselves:
team name, contact, sport, deadline, shipping address, and the items
with sizes and quantities, picked from your shop's real price list. When
they submit, it lands in your queue **with your name on it**, exactly as
if you'd typed it in.

A few things worth knowing:

- It's the same link every time. Send it to as many customers as you
  like — you don't need a new one per customer.
- Orders that come in this way are flagged as customer-entered, so the
  office knows nobody on staff has checked the details yet. Give them a
  read before they go to production.
- The customer never signs in and never sees pricing beyond your own
  price list, or anything about any other order.

If you don't see the card on your home screen, your shop's plan doesn't
include this yet.

## Customer tracking link

Every order (once submitted) has a **Customer tracking link** — a
no-login page you can text or email to the customer. It shows:

- The order's current status (Order received → Design in progress →
  Design approved → In production → Shipped).
- The mockup itself, once posted, with Approve / Request changes buttons
  (see "Reviewing a mockup" above).
- Any shipping tracking numbers the office has added, each a clickable
  link to that carrier's own tracking page.
- Your shop's terms — payment, turnaround, tax and shipping — if the
  office has filled them in.

Tap **Copy** next to the link on the order page to grab it. No pricing,
contact info, or cost data ever shows on this page — it's safe to hand to
anyone.

## Order receipt

**View / print receipt** on the order page opens a clean, customer-safe
receipt — items, sizes, quantities, pricing, and balance due, with a
**Print / Save as PDF** button. If your shop has filled in its terms
(payment terms, turnaround time, tax and shipping), those print on the
receipt too, so you don't have to explain them separately. Nothing about cost, vendor, or profit ever
shows here. Use this for an actual paper or emailed receipt; it's a
different page from the tracking link above (that one's for status, this
one's for the money side).

## Finding your orders

On **My Orders** you can:

- Search by team name, order #, or your own name.
- Sort by soonest deadline, newest, or largest total.
- Filter by a date range (by month).
- Show/hide cancelled orders.

## Reordering

On any of your own past orders, tap **Reorder** to start a brand-new order
pre-filled with the same details — handy for a repeat team ordering again.

## Reporting a problem

See something broken, confusing, or just wrong anywhere in the app? Tap the
**Report Issue** button in the bottom-right corner of any screen, describe
what happened, and submit. It goes straight to the developer — no other
step needed on your end.
